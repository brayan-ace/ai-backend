## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Storage Locations

Your data is stored on:

- **Firebase Firestore** (Google Cloud)
- **Railway** (Cloud hosting)
- **Local device storage** (cached data)
- **Backup servers** (for redundancy)

Data may be stored in multiple geographic locations for redundancy and disaster recovery.

### 4.2 Data Retention

#### Active Account

- Account and profile information: Retained as long as account is active
- Chat history: Retained per your preferences; default retention is configurable
- Learning analytics: Retained for duration of account
- Payment data: Retained per payment processor requirements

#### After Account Deletion

- Personal data is deleted within 30-90 days
- Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained indefinitely
- Data may be retained longer if required by law
- Backup copies may persist for technical recovery purposes
- You may specifically request permanent deletion

#### Legal Retention

- Data may be retained longer if required by law
- Investigation purposes may require extended retention
- Compliance requirements may mandate retention
- Court orders may prevent deletion

### 4.3 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

#### Technical Safeguards

- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- HTTPS/TLS for data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- Password hashing with salting
- Regular security audits and testing
- Intrusion detection and prevention
- Firewalls and DDoS protection
- Regular software updates and patches

#### Administrative Safeguards

- Role-based access controls
- Principle of least privilege
- Employee confidentiality agreements
- Access logging and monitoring
- Regular staff training on security
- Background checks for employees

#### Physical Safeguards

- Secure data center facilities
- Access controls and surveillance
- Environmental protections
- Disaster recovery plans
- Business continuity procedures

### 4.4 Security Limitations

While we implement reasonable security measures, **no system is completely secure**. We cannot guarantee:

- Absolute prevention of unauthorized access
- Protection against all types of cyberattacks
- Immunity from data breaches
- Perfect system reliability

We urge you to take precautions:

- Use strong, unique passwords
- Do not share login credentials
- Use secure networks
- Report suspicious activity immediately
- Keep your device software updated
